How the method evolved

We triedWhat went wrongWhat we did
Top 25% of balls, raw strike rate and economyMostly luck, especially for bowlersCompared with an average player in the same situation; weighted balls by pressure
"Pressure minus usual level" scoreRepeatability below zero: pure noiseUsed the usual level only as a starting estimate and show it as evidence
Rank on the average aloneOne hot streak lifts a player too highPenalise uncertainty (rating = adjusted impact minus half the uncertainty)
One pressure index onlyMisses knockouts, collapses, set battersAdded 13 match-situation tests with 30% weight

Limits to keep in mind

  • Places 6 to 10 on both lists are close calls; treat them as one group.
  • Knockout evidence is the thinnest: very few matches and a few balls per player.
  • IPL form and T20I form are only weakly linked; players rated mostly on T20Is faced weaker opposition on average.
  • Not modelled: batter-bowler matchups, pitch and venue, fielding, fitness and age. Availability and retirement are ignored on purpose: this is a performance ranking.
  • The explainer uses a simplified win-probability formula so it can run in your browser; the ratings use the full model.
